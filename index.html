Value-at-Risk and Expected Shortfall of a Four-Stock Portfolio

MFIN 354, Risk Management in Financial Institutions, Summer 2026

  • Tested for ARCH effects and fitted GARCH, GJR-GARCH, EGARCH and EWMA volatility models on four US equities (NEM, SLB, ROST, MAS) over 2012–2026, finding a significant leverage effect in portfolio volatility.
  • Estimated 1-day 99% VaR and ES using nine methods. Parametric models clustered at $330K–$380K, while stressed VaR over the COVID window was about 2.8 times the historical-simulation figure ($1.05M).

Predicting Consumer Loan Default Risk

MFIN 350D, Data Science for Finance, Summer 2026

  • Built logistic regression and random forest models on 32,000+ loan records using a leakage-safe pipeline, with multicollinearity tested through VIF.
  • Showed the default 0.50 cutoff misses too many defaulters. Lowering it to 0.33 catches 257 more defaults, and we recommended the explainable logistic model for regulated lending.
